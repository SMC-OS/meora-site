MeOra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Publisher: Nuvora Development

1. About this policy

MeOra (“MeOra”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a mobile application published by Nuvora Development. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and protected when you use MeOra and related services.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you create or use a MeOra account, we may process your email address, internal user identifier, account status and authentication-related information needed to securely sign you in. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not stored by MeOra in readable form.

App information and preferences

We may store app preferences, notification preferences, saved app state or progress, and feature usage information where needed to provide and personalise the service.

Subscriptions and purchases

Google Play processes Android purchases. MeOra may receive product identifiers, transaction or purchase identifiers, subscription status, renewal or expiry information and entitlement status. MeOra does not receive or store your full payment-card details. RevenueCat is used to manage subscription and entitlement status.

Analytics and diagnostics

PostHog is used for product analytics and feature-usage measurement. Depending on configuration, analytics may include app interactions, timestamps, app version, operating system, device characteristics and pseudonymous or account-linked identifiers. Sentry is used for crash reporting, error diagnostics and reliability monitoring and may process technical information such as error messages, stack traces, app version, operating system and device information.

Notifications and technical data

If you enable notifications, MeOra may process a push notification token and notification settings. Infrastructure and service providers may also process technical information such as IP address, app version, operating system, timestamps, device or app identifiers and network-related information for security, service delivery and troubleshooting.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide and operate MeOra, authenticate users, maintain accounts, personalise features, deliver notifications, verify premium access and subscriptions, provide support, analyse product usage, diagnose technical problems, improve reliability, protect against abuse or fraud, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

MeOra does not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for third-party behavioural advertising.

4. Service providers

MeOra uses third-party service providers that process information as part of delivering the service. Supabase provides backend infrastructure, authentication and database services. RevenueCat provides subscription, purchase-verification and entitlement management. Google Play processes Android purchases and distributes the app. PostHog provides product analytics and feature-usage measurement. Sentry provides crash reporting, error monitoring and diagnostics.

5. International processing

Some service providers may process information in countries outside the country where you live. Where applicable law requires it, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers of personal information.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide MeOra, maintain security and reliability, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. When you request account deletion, account-linked information is deleted or anonymised except where retention is reasonably necessary for legal, fraud-prevention, security, accounting, dispute-resolution or regulatory purposes. Some information may remain temporarily in encrypted backups until those backups expire through their normal retention cycle.

To request deletion, see our Delete Account page.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including where UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection to certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

MeOra is not designed or directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law. If we learn that such information has been collected contrary to applicable requirements, we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

10. Medical and wellbeing disclaimer

MeOra provides motivational and wellbeing-related content and tools. It is not a medical service and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, emergency assistance or professional healthcare advice. If you require medical or mental-health assistance, contact an appropriate qualified professional or emergency service.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as MeOra develops or as legal, technical or operational requirements change. The “Last updated” date will be revised when changes are made.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, rights requests or account-deletion assistance, email support@meora.app.
