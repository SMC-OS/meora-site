Frequently asked questions

Trouble signing in

Check that you are using the email address linked to your MeOra account and retry the sign-in flow. If the issue continues, contact support.

Notifications not arriving

Check MeOra notification settings and your device notification permissions. Battery-saving or system settings may also restrict delivery.

Premium or subscription not showing

Make sure you are signed in to the correct MeOra and Google Play accounts. MeOra uses RevenueCat to verify premium entitlement status from Google Play purchases.

Restore purchases

Use the restore-purchases option in MeOra when available. If premium access still does not appear, contact support with the email address linked to your account.

Cancel a Google Play subscription

Android subscription billing and cancellation are managed by Google Play. Cancelling your subscription does not automatically delete your MeOra account.

Delete account

Use MeOra → Settings → Account → Delete account, or use the external process on our Delete Account page.

Report a bug

Email support@meora.app and include what happened, what you expected, your device model and app version if available. Do not send passwords or private security credentials.
