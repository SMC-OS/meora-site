MeOra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Publisher: Nuvora Development

1. About this policy

MeOra is a personal wellbeing app published by Nuvora Development (“we”, “us”). It offers daily affirmations, reflection prompts, a private journal, favourites, collections, reminders and optional Premium features. This policy explains what information MeOra handles and the choices you have. You can read a shorter version inside the app under Settings.

2. What stays on your phone

Most of what makes MeOra personal never leaves your phone: your answers to the setup questions (what you would like more of, how you have been feeling, areas of life you want support with, tone, motivations, themes you would rather not see, visual mood, and the optional age range and star sign), your first name if you give one, your language, theme and background choices, your daily progress and streak, your favourites, history, collections and your own affirmations, your “more/less like this” choices and your check-in answers.

Your journal is saved on your phone every time. It is kept in MeOra’s private app storage and is not separately encrypted by MeOra, so it relies on your phone’s own protections such as its screen lock.

3. Account information

Using MeOra without an account is possible. If you sign in, we process your email address, an internal user identifier and the authentication information needed to sign you in securely. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not stored by MeOra in readable form.

When you are signed in, MeOra may also save to your account in our database: journal entries, favourites, reminder settings, your streak, your Premium status and a small setup snapshot (such as your chosen theme). Access to this data is limited to your own account by database access rules. It is protected in transit and by our hosting provider’s standard protections. Your journal and other saved data are not end-to-end encrypted.

The personal answers you give during setup and in “Your MeOra” (feelings, goals, tone, age range, star sign, themes you hide and similar) are not sent to your account or to us.

4. Purchases

Premium is bought through Google Play. Google handles your payment details; MeOra never sees your card number. We use RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous user identifier and purchase information such as product, status and renewal or expiry dates. Your Premium status is confirmed on our server before Premium features unlock.

5. Analytics and crash reports

Analytics (PostHog). MeOra sends anonymous product events such as “daily set completed”, “topic followed” or “card shared”, with simple counts and public content identifiers, plus technical details such as app version and device type. We never send your name, email, journal text, your own affirmations, setup answers, search text or any free text. You can switch analytics off at any time in Settings → Privacy & data.

Crash reports (Sentry). If the app fails, technical details such as the app version, device model, operating-system version and the location of the error are sent so we can fix the problem. Messages and text that could contain personal content are removed before sending. You can switch crash reports off in Settings → Privacy & data.

These are two separate choices: switching one off does not switch off the other. Both are on by default.

6. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone, and only after you turn them on. They contain a short public MeOra thought, never your journal, your name or your setup answers. MeOra does not use a push-notification server. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in your phone’s notification settings.

7. Permissions and sharing

MeOra asks for notification permission only when you choose a reminder. It does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts, and it does not read your photos. When you share a card, the picture is created on your phone and handed to the app you choose; we do not upload it. If a share link is included, it is the public MeOra website address.

8. Sensitive wellbeing information

Some of your answers can say something about your wellbeing. We treat them as sensitive: they stay on your phone, are not used for advertising, are not shared with analytics, crash reporting or our purchase provider, and can be edited, exported or deleted by you at any time. MeOra is a wellbeing companion, not a medical service.

9. Service providers

Supabase provides sign-in and database services. RevenueCat and Google Play provide subscription and purchase services. PostHog provides product analytics. Sentry provides crash reporting. Vercel hosts this website. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

10. International processing

Our providers may process information in countries outside the one where you live, including outside the UK and the European Economic Area. Where the law requires it, recognised safeguards for international transfers are used.

11. Keeping and deleting information

Information on your phone stays until you delete it (Settings → Privacy & data) or uninstall the app. If you have an account, your account data stays until you delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete account). Deleting your account removes your account and the data saved to it from our database. Copies held in provider backups, if any, expire on the provider’s normal cycle. Subscription and payment records remain with Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies. See Delete your account.

12. Your rights

You can change or clear your answers in Settings → Your MeOra, export your data from Settings → Privacy & data, delete data on your phone, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, including where UK or EU data-protection law applies, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, port or object to our use of your information, and to complain to your data-protection authority. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

13. Children

MeOra is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.

15. Contact

For privacy questions, rights requests or help with deletion, email support@meora.app.
