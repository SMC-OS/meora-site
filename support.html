Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can use MeOra as a guest. An account lets you keep journal entries and favourites across devices.

Trouble signing in

Use the email address linked to your MeOra account and try again. If the problem continues, email us.

Premium or subscription not showing

Make sure you are signed in to the same Google account that bought it, then open Premium and tap Restore purchases. If it still does not appear, email us from the address linked to your account.

Cancel a Google Play subscription

Subscriptions are managed in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Cancelling does not delete your MeOra account.

Notifications not arriving

Check Settings → Reminders in MeOra and your phone’s notification and battery-saving settings.

Change language, theme or backgrounds

Settings → Language (eight languages), Theme and Quote backgrounds. Your choice stays until you change it.

Export or delete my data

Settings → Privacy & data lets you export a copy, clear parts of your data or delete everything on your phone. To delete your account, use Settings → Account → Delete account, or see Delete account.

Is my journal private?

It is saved on your phone and never sent to analytics or crash reports. If you are signed in, entries are also saved to your account; they are protected by access rules but are not end-to-end encrypted.

Report a bug

Email us what happened, what you expected, your device model and app version. Please do not send passwords.

Wellbeing support

MeOra is not therapy or emergency support. If you are struggling or in danger, contact a qualified professional or your local emergency or crisis service.
