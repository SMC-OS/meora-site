Account controls

Delete your MeOra account

You can request permanent deletion of your MeOra account and associated personal data.

Delete inside MeOra

Open MeOra → Settings → Account → Delete account and follow the instructions shown. If the label differs slightly in your current app version, use the account or privacy section to locate the delete option.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the email address associated with your MeOra account and use the subject MeOra Account Deletion Request.

Email account deletion request

What we delete

After a valid deletion request, we delete or anonymise the account profile and authentication record, app preferences and user-linked application data, subject to technical and legal exceptions described below.

What may be retained

Limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal, fraud-prevention, security, dispute, accounting or regulatory reasons. Temporary encrypted backups may also persist until they expire through their normal retention cycle.

Google Play subscriptions are separate

Deleting your MeOra account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Manage subscription cancellation separately through Google Play.

For more information about how MeOra handles data, read the Privacy Policy.
