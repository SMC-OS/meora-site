Account controls

Delete your MeOra account

You can permanently delete your MeOra account and the data saved to it.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Delete inside MeOra

Open MeOra → Settings → Account → Delete account, confirm that you understand it cannot be undone, and tap Delete my account. The deletion happens immediately, you are signed out, and MeOra data on that phone is cleared.

If you cannot open the app

Email us from the address linked to your MeOra account with the subject MeOra Account Deletion Request. We verify that the request comes from the account holder, then delete the account without undue delay and within the time the law requires.

Email deletion request

What is deleted

Your sign-in record and profile, your journal entries saved to the account, favourites, streak, reminder settings, the setup snapshot, and your Premium status record and billing identity in our database. Everything saved only on your phone (setup answers, history, collections, your own affirmations and journal entries kept locally) is cleared from that phone as part of the in-app deletion. You can also export your data first from Settings → Privacy & data.

What may remain

Purchase and subscription records stay with Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies, because they relate to a payment. Provider backups, if any, expire on the provider’s normal cycle. We may keep limited records where the law requires it or to deal with fraud, security or disputes.

Google Play subscriptions are separate

Deleting your MeOra account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

More detail is in the Privacy Policy.
